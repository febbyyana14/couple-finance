## Brand & Style

This design system balances private wealth management with domestic partnership clarity. Designed for couples seeking financial synchronicity, it eliminates the clinical coldness of enterprise fintech in favor of a warm, architectural, and editorial atmosphere.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Private Wealth Editorial:** Employs an authoritative serif for key numeric totals and section titles, paired with a modern, high-legibility geometric sans-serif for operational telemetry.
- **Warm Architectural Canvas:** The interface relies on warm ivory backgrounds and deep vintage teal anchors rather than stark monochromatic grays.
- **Collaborative Parity:** Visual symmetry guarantees that shared holdings, individual commitments, and distinct profiles receive equal weight without friction.

### Emotional Experience
- **Reassurance:** High-contrast, diffused-elevation containers offer visual stability.
- **Calm Mastery:** Financial stress is mitigated through subdued categorical accents, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and clean metric cards.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic contrast between deep, anchoring teal tones and an illuminated, warm ivory workspace. Semantic colors are mapped directly to financial balance vectors:

- **Primary (`#134E48`) & Secondary (`#0E4D43`):** Define the core navigation architecture, structural frames, and primary interactive buttons.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F2`):** Serves as the primary viewport surface, softening glare and enhancing extended session comfort.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces for optimal data scanning and sharp contrast against `#FAF7F2`.
- **Panel Border (`#E5E0D8`):** Subtle tactile line separations that organize high-density dashboards without visual clutter.

### Semantic & Allocation Accents
- **Emerald Teal (`#10B981`):** Net Worth, savings deposits, and positive asset trajectories.
- **Ocean Blue (`#0284C7`):** Liquid cash, checking accounts, and recurring income flows.
- **Terracotta Coral (`#EA580C`):** Outgoing expenses, debt liabilities, and category spending caps exceeding 90%.
- **Amber Vintage (`#D97706`):** Pending commitments, upcoming utility schedules, and 75–90% budget warnings.
- **Golden Glow (`#EAB308`):** Reached savings milestones, completed targets, and financial achievements.
- **Rose Crimson (`#E11D48`):** Critical alerts, past-due obligations, and over-budget notices.
- **Crypto Amber (`#F7931A`):** Alternative asset allocations and digital currency holdings.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial prestige of **Newsreader** with the structural clarity of **Manrope**.

- **Newsreader (Display, Headlines):** Conveys stability, heritage, and intentional stewardship. Used exclusively for top-line metrics, portfolio totals, and major analytical section headers.
- **Manrope (Body, Form Controls, Tables, Labels):** Delivers clean optical performance for complex ledgers, timestamp arrays, and fine-print audit trails.
- **Caps & Micro-Tracking:** Metric labels (`NET WORTH`, `CASH FLOW`) are rendered in `label-md` with `0.06em` tracking to provide crisp, scannable anchors above high-impact numerical values.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses an 8px modular spacing system, organized around a responsive 12-column fluid grid.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** Fixed 280px left sidebar anchor with a flexible multi-column workspace. The KPI row spans 4 equal columns. Detailed analytics display across a 2-column split (6 cols each).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** Sidebar collapses into an icon rail or full-height off-canvas drawer. The KPI grid adapts to a 2x2 matrix, with analytics stacking sequentially.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout with strict 1rem (16px) margins. Metric cards convert to a horizontal scroll snap strip or single-column stacked pills.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Internal Component Padding:** High-density forms and tables utilize `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px).
- **Surface Containment:** Cards and modal dialogs use `space-lg` (24px) to preserve breathing room around dense numerical datasets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined physical layering model: low-opacity, deep-tinted ambient shadows paired with crisp 1px borders.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base background `#FAF7F2`, completely un-elevated.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border of `#E5E0D8` and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(14, 77, 67, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Popovers & Flyouts):** Lifted surfaces featuring `0 10px 28px -4px rgba(14, 77, 67, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Drawers):** Floating overlays against a dimmed backdrop (`rgba(14, 77, 67, 0.40)` with `blur(4px)`), elevated by `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(14, 77, 67, 0.16)`.

Shadows are tinted with the deep teal brand seed (`rgba(14, 77, 67, *)`) rather than neutral black, preserving warmth and cohesion across ivory surfaces.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Roundedness 2**:
- **Default Radius (`rounded-md`):** `0.5rem` (8px) applied to inputs, table cell selections, buttons, and inline badges.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) applied to internal chart containers, nested sub-panels, and profile switchers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for dashboard summary cards, savings goal containers, and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for contextual category chips, user role badges, and status pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Teal (`#134E48`) fill, white text, 8px radius (`rounded-md`), Manrope SemiBold 14px. Hover transitions to `#0E4D43`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with 1px `#E5E0D8` border, deep teal text, subtle `#FAF7F2` background on hover.
- **Icon Actions:** 36x36px square with 8px radius for quick actions (`+ Setor`, `- Tarik`), matching the container's contextual accent color.

### Metric Cards (KPI Display)
- High-contrast pure white card with `rounded-xl` borders and teal-tinted elevation.
- Top row: Uppercase `label-md` category title alongside a circular Lucide icon badge with 10% opacity tint.
- Center: Large display value in `Newsreader` SemiBold.
- Bottom: Mini trend indicator (`+12% vs last month`) or subtext progress note in `body-sm`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Shared / "Bersama":** Deep teal background tint (`rgba(19, 78, 72, 0.1)`), deep teal text.
- **Husband / "Suami":** Ocean blue tint (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.1)`), ocean blue text.
- **Wife / "Istri":** Amber tint (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)`), amber vintage text.
- **Pill Shape:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), padded 4px vertical by 10px horizontal.

### Form Inputs
- 1px border in `#E5E0D8` on white background with 8px radius.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#134E48` with a 2px outer ring in `rgba(19, 78, 72, 0.15)`.
- Currency prefix (`Rp`, `$`) rendered in bold Manrope with a subtle vertical divider.

### Target Savings & Progress Bars
- 8px high progress tracks with `rounded-full` contours and `#E5E0D8` trough fills.
- Active fill dynamically transitions based on completion tiers: Ocean Blue (<50%), Emerald Teal (50–99%), Golden Glow (100% Target Met).

### Profile Switcher
- Dual-pill segmented control positioned in the header or sidebar base.
- Active segment transitions with a 200ms ease, highlighting the selected owner with full card elevation.
